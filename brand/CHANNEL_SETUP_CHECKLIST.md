## Official platform sources

Consulted on `2026-08-17`:

| Platform | Requirement used | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | banner 2560×1440 recommended; minimum safe area guidance; upload max 6 MB | [YouTube channel branding](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525) |
| YouTube | 16:9 thumbnail; official guidance recommends high resolution and minimum width 640 | [YouTube custom thumbnails](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431) |
| Twitch | profile banner 1200×480; concentrate graphics on the left | [Twitch channel page setup](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/channel-page-setup) |
| Instagram | Reels between 1.91:1 and 9:16; minimum 720 px; cover recommendation 420×654 | [Instagram Reels size and aspect ratios](https://www.facebook.com/help/1038071743007909) |
| LinkedIn | personal profile background recommendation 1584×396 | [LinkedIn background image](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a564208) |

Platform specifications may change. Re-check before a future upload batch; do not convert them into canonical brand decisions.
